AI Art Generator

A native SwiftUI iOS & iPadOS app powered by Pollinations.ai. Create custom artwork with style presets, resolution controls, seed locking, and a built-in pull-out QWERTY keyboard.

Art Style Presets

Switch between Hyper Realistic, Anime, Cyberpunk, Watercolor, Oil Painting, and more.

Full Generation Control

Customize aspect ratios (1:1, 3:4, 4:3), resolution up to 1024px, negative prompts, and seed locks.

Custom Pull-Out Keyboard

Built-in on-screen QWERTY keyboard with symbols, paste button, and quick field switching.
